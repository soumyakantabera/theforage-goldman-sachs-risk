# Goldman Sachs Risk Virtual Experience

**Program:** Risk Management Simulation for Operations & Credit Analyst Roles  
**Focus:** Identifying, categorizing, and mitigating risks in daily life and financial contexts — core to Goldman Sachs' risk culture.

---

## Task 1: Managing Risk in Your Daily Life

### What You'll Learn
- How to systematically identify, categorize (Market, Credit, Operational, Liquidity, Model, Enterprise Risk), and prioritize risks in everyday scenarios.
- Practical application of risk management frameworks used at Goldman Sachs to personal and professional situations.
- Critical thinking for mitigation strategies that reduce impact and likelihood.

### What You'll Do
- Review three relatable scenarios: getting to a 9:00 a.m. Monday exam, organizing a weekend road trip with friends, and planning an on-campus event.
- List and categorize all potential risks for each scenario.
- Reflect on how these mirror financial and operational risks at a global institution like Goldman Sachs.

### Input
- `Task1_Daily_Risk_Management/Input/image.png` — Detailed risk table with 5 categorized risks per scenario (Market Risk, Credit Risk, Operational Risk, Liquidity Risk, Model Risk, Enterprise Risk).

### Output
- `Task1_Daily_Risk_Management/Output/Daily_Life_Risk_Analysis.docx` — Comprehensive risk register expanding on the table with additional risks, impact assessments, and mitigation recommendations for each scenario.

**Key Insight:** This exercise demonstrates that risk identification is the foundation of operational resilience — whether for a student exam or a multi-billion-dollar trade settlement at Goldman Sachs.

---

## Task 2: Individual Credit Risk Assessment

### What You'll Learn
- Credit risk assessment fundamentals: evaluating default likelihood using credit history, repayment capacity, income stability, and financial buffers.
- How Goldman Sachs analysts use weighted models (Credit Score 25%, Savings 25%, Income/Debt 20% each, Late Payments 10%) to quantify risk.
- The impact of small changes in variables on overall risk category and decision-making.

### What You'll Do
- Input persona financial profiles into the simplified Risk Calculator.
- Analyze how different attributes drive High/Moderate/Low risk classifications.
- Reflect on real-world implications for lending, portfolio management, and client advisory at Goldman Sachs.

### Inputs
- `Task2_Credit_Risk_Assessment/Input/Risk Calculator.xlsx` — Weighted scoring template (defaults show High Risk; adjust with persona data).
- `Task2_Credit_Risk_Assessment/Input/Task 2 Individual credit risk.docx` — Four fictional personas with detailed financial profiles and notes.

### Output
- `Task2_Credit_Risk_Assessment/Output/Credit_Risk_Analysis_Report.docx` — Professional analysis of all four personas with risk categorization, key drivers, sensitivity insights, and Goldman Sachs context.

**Key Insight:** Credit risk is not static — proactive monitoring of savings-to-debt ratios, late payment history, and income stability allows Goldman Sachs to make sound decisions that protect both the firm and clients.

## Work Results Summary

### Task 1 — Daily Life Risks Identified & Categorized

| Scenario                        | # Risks Listed | Dominant Risk Types                  | Top Mitigation Themes                     | GS Parallel                          |
|---------------------------------|----------------|--------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| 9:00 a.m. Monday Exam          | 5+            | Operational, Market, Liquidity, Model | Backup plans, time buffers, digital tools | Trade settlement timing & contingency |
| Weekend Road Trip              | 5+            | Credit, Operational, Market, Liquidity| Shared costs agreements, insurance, route planning | Counterparty risk & liquidity events |
| On-Campus Event                | 5+            | Operational, Market, Credit, Enterprise | Vendor contracts, attendance forecasting, budget reserves | Event-driven market/operational risk |

### Task 2 — Credit Risk Assessment Results

| Persona | Profile Summary                          | Calculated Risk Level | Primary Risk Drivers                     | Key Recommendation for GS Analyst     |
|---------|------------------------------------------|-----------------------|------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------|
| 1       | Entrepreneur: $280k income, 760 score, $450k savings, $150k debt, 0 late payments | Low-Moderate         | Strong savings buffer & credit history   | Portfolio diversification advisory    |
| 2       | Professional: $180k income, 700 score, $120k savings, $100k debt, 1 late payment   | Moderate             | Single late payment + moderate debt      | Mortgage + debt consolidation review  |
| 3       | IT Consultant: $100k income, 660 score, $20k savings, $90k debt, 2 late payments   | High                 | Low savings, multiple late payments      | Debt restructuring + credit repair plan |
| 4       | Business Owner: $150k income, 720 score, $80k savings, $200k debt, 1 late payment  | Moderate-High        | High debt-to-savings ratio               | Cash flow management & seasonal buffer |

**Overall Finding:** Savings and credit score carry the highest weights (0.25 each). Small improvements in late payments or savings can shift a profile from High to Moderate risk — a key insight for client counseling at Goldman Sachs.
